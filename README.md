# ESIA: Four-Laning of the Dhaka–Sylhet Highway (N2)

An Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) with an Environmental and Social Management Plan (ESMP) and Resettlement Action Plan framework for the proposed four-laning of the 214 km Dhaka–Sylhet National Highway, Bangladesh.

> **Note:** This is an illustrative academic report prepared as a university assignment (EWCE-431), based on publicly known characteristics of the N2 corridor. It is not a certified environmental clearance document, and the consultations described are illustrative.

## Project Summary

The Roads and Highways Department (RHD) proposes to upgrade the two-lane N2 into a four-lane divided carriageway from Dhaka (Kanchpur) to Sylhet, with six town bypasses, four grade-separated interchanges and bridge works, including a new parallel bridge at Bhairab. The project falls under the **Red Category** of the Environment Conservation Rules.

## Headline Findings

| Topic | Finding |
|---|---|
| Land take | About 1,540 ha permanent acquisition |
| People affected | About 8,500–9,500 households, of which 900–1,100 face physical displacement |
| Noise | Operational levels of 68–74 dB(A) at 15 m exceed residential standards |
| Biodiversity | 3–4 km stretch beside Satchari National Park; 3,800–4,500 roadside trees affected |
| Traffic | Level of Service improves from D–F to B–C; travel time falls from about 6 h to under 4 h |
| ESMP cost | Indicative 3–5% of civil works cost |

**Key mitigation:** avoid incursion into the park boundary, build wildlife crossings and fencing, replant trees at a minimum 3:1 ratio, follow a "no displacement before compensation" principle, and enforce access management along the new highway.
